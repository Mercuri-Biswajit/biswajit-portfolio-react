## 2024-05-30 - Prevent focus ring clipping on `overflow: hidden` interactive containers
**Learning:** In complex interactive cards using `tabIndex={0}` and `overflow: hidden` (like `ProjectCard` and `HomeProjectCard`), the browser's default `:focus-visible` outline is often clipped or invisible. Additionally, relying on generic global reset focus styles is insufficient for deep component architecture.
**Action:** Always apply `outline: none;` on the outer card's `:focus-visible` state and explicitly apply `outline: Xpx solid var(--color-accent); outline-offset: Ypx;` to an inner wrapper (like `.project-card-inner`) or directly if padding/offset allows. Use the specific interactive elements' `:focus-visible` pseudoclass for explicit accessibility cues rather than relying on global defaults.
